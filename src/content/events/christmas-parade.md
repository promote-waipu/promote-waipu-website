## Waipu Christmas Eve Parade

The Waipu Christmas Eve Parade is one of the most beloved events on the Northland calendar, drawing spectacular crowds from across the region every year on 24 December.

The parade winds through the centre of the village with floats, music, community groups and plenty of festive cheer. A wonderful family evening that captures everything that's great about the Waipu community.

### Details

* **When:** Christmas Eve — 24 December annually
* **Where:** Waipu Village, The Centre
* **Cost:** Free to attend
* **Parking:** Available at the showgrounds and surrounding streets

Keep an eye on our Facebook page and events calendar for the 2026 start time and float entry information.
